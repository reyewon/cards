# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Adults (18+) playing together in person on one phone, or apart on two phones via a room code. Context: evenings, drinks, low light, one hand on the phone. Ryan's audience comes via his Rysta persona (Instagram @rystadom, FetLife RYSTA), so many players arrive already in a flirty, kink-positive mindset. The job: keep a session flowing with zero friction, every tap from the sofa.

## Product Purpose

A kinky question-and-forfeit card game with three modes (couple, friends, group), intensity levels, and remote multiplayer. Success: sessions that run long, players returning and noticing fresh cards (the batch system), and the game feeling like a physical deck on a table rather than a web page.

## Brand Personality

Cheeky, confident, unashamed. British party energy with a boudoir undertone: late-night parlour, not neon arcade. The bubbly sticker wordmark with the devil face is the brand anchor; hot pink is the brand colour.

## Anti-references

- The 2023 "AI dark mode" look: cyan-on-dark neon, purple gradients, glassmorphism, floating particle dots.
- Corporate quiz apps (Kahoot brightness) and childish candy styling.
- Sterile SaaS component styling; this is a game, the cards must feel like objects.

## Design Principles

- The card is the hero: every screen exists to deliver the next card, and the card should feel dealt, not rendered.
- UI chrome disappears; expressive type is spent only on game moments (turn calls, reveals, the deal).
- One brand colour spent deliberately: pink means action and mischief, never decoration everywhere.
- Thumb-first: everything reachable and tappable one-handed on a phone in the dark.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text (dark room, dimmed screens make this stricter, not looser). Full reduced-motion alternatives. No colour-only state signalling. Touch targets at least 44px.
